# Picture Imperfect

Picture Imperfect is a short narrative browser game created for the GMTK game jam and Hack Club's Jame Gam.

The player's main goal is to prepare their family for a picture in only a few seconds. At first, it seems the main job is to make the picture look "as perfect as possible." However, as the game continues, strange objects, hidden memories, and a mysterious figure begins to appear in the room, making the player question if that really is the *true* goal.

The player's choices determine which of the five endings they receive.

# Story

The player is part of a family that are preparing to leave their old home. Before leaving, they decide to take one last picture.

The narrator, aka the photographer, believes that a perfect photograph can preserve the moment forever. However, each new photo reveals that the house is holding tons of memories that the family has forgotten.

# Planned Gameplay

- A tutorial test photo round
- Timed 'fix the picture' rounds
- Clickable "problems" inside the room
- Character and object reactions
- Hidden player-choice scores
- A 'mysterious figure' appearing in pictures
- Five endings
- A final photo album
